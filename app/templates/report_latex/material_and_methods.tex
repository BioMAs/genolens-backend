\subsection{Input Data}
\JINJABLOCK{if m.imported}
The count matrix behind the results, and the filtering of its samples and genes, are not described here.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
The analysis was performed on the provided gene-level count matrix.
\JINJABLOCK{if m.qc.min_reads is not none and m.qc.min_genes is not none}
A sample was kept when it had at least \JINJAVAR{m.qc.min_reads|fmt_g} total counts and at least \JINJAVAR{m.qc.min_genes|fmt_g} genes with a count above zero.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
The minimum sequencing depth and the minimum number of detected genes required of a sample are not recorded for this analysis.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{if m.qc.removed_sample_ids is none}
The samples excluded are not recorded.
\JINJABLOCK{elif m.qc.removed_sample_ids}
\JINJABLOCK{set n_removed = m.qc.removed_sample_ids|length}
\JINJAVAR{n_removed} \JINJAVAR{'sample was' if n_removed == 1 else 'samples were'} excluded: \JINJAVAR{m.qc.removed_sample_ids|map('tex_escape')|join(', ')}.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
No sample was excluded.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{if m.qc.relaxed}
Fewer than two samples passed these thresholds, so they were relaxed: the two samples with the largest library sizes were kept.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{if m.min_count is not none}
Genes with very low expression were then removed: a gene was kept when its raw counts (before any normalisation) reached \JINJAVAR{m.min_count|fmt_g} in \JINJABLOCK{if m.min_reps is not none}at least \JINJAVAR{m.min_reps|fmt_g} samples\JINJABLOCK{else}a number of samples that is not recorded\JINJABLOCK{endif}\JINJABLOCK{if m.min_reps_mode == 'auto'} (the number of samples of the smallest compared group; above ten samples per group the requirement is reduced to $10 + 0.7 (n - 10)$)\JINJABLOCK{endif}.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
The filter that removes genes with very low expression is not recorded for this analysis.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{endif}

\subsection{Differential Gene Expression Analysis}
\JINJABLOCK{if m.imported}

\JINJABLOCK{if m.analysis_gone}
The analysis that produced these results is no longer available; its methods are not described here.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
The differential analysis was not run in GenoLens; its methods are not described here.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
The calls (up- and down-regulated genes), the log$_2$ fold-changes and the adjusted \textit{p}-values are shown as they \JINJAVAR{'are stored' if m.analysis_gone else 'were imported'} with the dataset; the statistical model and the versions of the software that produced them are not reported.
\JINJABLOCK{else}

\JINJABLOCK{if m.primary_method == 'DESeq2'}
Differentially expressed genes (DEGs) were called with \textbf{DESeq2} (Love et al., 2014), which fits a negative binomial generalised linear model with shrinkage of the dispersion estimates and tests each gene with a Wald test. Its log$_2$ fold-changes are maximum-likelihood estimates (unshrunken).
\JINJABLOCK{elif m.primary_method == 'edgeR'}
Differentially expressed genes (DEGs) were called with \textbf{edgeR} (Robinson et al., 2010), which fits a negative binomial model on TMM-normalised counts and performs quasi-likelihood F-tests to account for overdispersion.
\JINJABLOCK{elif m.primary_method == 'limma'}
Differentially expressed genes (DEGs) were called with \textbf{limma-voom} (Ritchie et al., 2015), which models log-transformed data with precision weights derived from the mean--variance trend and employs empirical Bayes shrinkage of variance estimates.
\JINJABLOCK{elif m.primary_by_comparison}
The method that decided the differentially expressed gene (DEG) calls \JINJAVAR{'differs between the comparisons' if m.primary_differs else 'is not recorded for every comparison'}:
\JINJABLOCK{for name, method in m.primary_by_comparison}
\JINJABLOCK{if name}\JINJAVAR{name|tex_escape}, \JINJABLOCK{endif}\JINJAVAR{method}\JINJAVAR{'; ' if not loop.last else '.'}
\JINJABLOCK{endfor}
\JINJABLOCK{else}
The method that decided the differentially expressed gene (DEG) calls is not recorded for this analysis.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{if m.ahead_methods}
\JINJAVAR{m.ahead_methods|join(' and ')} gave no result, so \JINJAVAR{m.primary_method|method_name} decided the calls.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{if m.primary_method or m.primary_by_comparison}
P-values were corrected for multiple testing using the Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) False Discovery Rate.
\JINJABLOCK{if m.fdr is not none and m.min_log2fc is not none}
A gene was classified as a DEG if (i) its adjusted \textit{p}-value was below \JINJAVAR{m.fdr|fmt_g} and (ii) the absolute value of its log$_2$ fold-change was at or above \JINJAVAR{m.min_log2fc|fmt_g}.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
The thresholds on the adjusted \textit{p}-value and on the log$_2$ fold-change that define a DEG are not recorded for this analysis.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{endif}

\JINJABLOCK{if m.other_methods}
\JINJAVAR{m.other_methods|join(' and ')} were also run on the same counts; they do not decide the calls and are not reported here:

\begin{enumerate}
\JINJABLOCK{for other in m.other_methods}
\JINJABLOCK{if other == 'DESeq2'}
  \item \textbf{DESeq2} (Love et al., 2014): fits a negative binomial generalised linear model with shrinkage of the dispersion estimates and tests each gene with a Wald test.
\JINJABLOCK{elif other == 'edgeR'}
  \item \textbf{edgeR} (Robinson et al., 2010): fits a negative binomial model on TMM-normalised counts and performs quasi-likelihood F-tests to account for overdispersion.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
  \item \textbf{limma-voom} (Ritchie et al., 2015): models log-transformed data with precision weights derived from the mean--variance trend and employs empirical Bayes shrinkage of variance estimates.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{endfor}
\end{enumerate}

\JINJABLOCK{endif}

\subsection{Functional Enrichment Analysis}
\JINJABLOCK{if enr.kind in ['annodb', 'mixed']}

Functional enrichment analysis was performed by over-representation analysis (ORA) against the annotation database \JINJABLOCK{if enr.annodb_file}(file \texttt{\JINJAVAR{enr.annodb_file|tex_escape}})\JINJABLOCK{else}(file not recorded)\JINJABLOCK{endif}, separately for the up-regulated and for the down-regulated DEGs.
\JINJABLOCK{if enr.categories}
The categories tested were \JINJAVAR{enr.categories|map('tex_escape')|join(', ')}.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
No selection of categories was made: the test was run over all categories of the annotation database.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}

\textbf{Over-representation analysis (ORA)} tested whether DEGs were over-represented in each term with a hypergeometric test; within each category, \textit{p}-values were adjusted by the Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) procedure.
\JINJABLOCK{if enr.fdr is not none}
A term was reported when at least three DEGs were annotated to it, its raw \textit{p}-value was below 0.05 and its adjusted \textit{p}-value was below \JINJAVAR{enr.fdr|fmt_g}.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
A term was reported when at least three DEGs were annotated to it, its raw \textit{p}-value was below 0.05 and its adjusted \textit{p}-value was below a cut-off that is not recorded for this analysis.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{if enr.background}
The background was the set of \JINJAVAR{enr.background|tex_escape}.
\JINJABLOCK{else}
The background set is not recorded for this analysis.
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
\JINJABLOCK{endif}
